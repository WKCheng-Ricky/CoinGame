NotoSans-SemiBold-subset.woff2 and NotoSans-Bold-subset.woff2 are subsets of the Noto Sans SemiBold and Bold
fonts (version 2.015), cut down to letters, digits, the pound sign and a space with the subset-font tool. They
are a Modified Version under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and carry no Reserved Font Name. The font's own name
table still holds the copyright (ID 0) and the licence notice and URL (IDs 13 and 14; the full text is OFL.txt); subset-font was run with
preserveNameIds 0 to 14 so subsetting does not strip them.

The pages load them as the "CoinLetters" family, only for the lettering on the coins, so the coin wording is
laid out against known glyph shapes (e2e/lettering.spec.js measures them). The Noto and Google names are not
used to promote this app.
